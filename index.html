How it works

No waiting for delivery. You can be coloring within minutes.

  1. Buy and download the PDF from Gumroad.
  2. Print at 100% on US Letter (8.5 x 11 in). On A4, choose "Fit to Page".
  3. Print single-sided. Use thicker paper for markers.
  4. Color, tick the box, and enjoy!

About the studio

Original printable activities for children and families.

Shahin Creative Studio is an independent digital design studio run by Md Shahin Alam, based in Bangladesh. The studio creates simple, cheerful printables that give children a screen-free way to be creative.

Every book is designed with clarity first: clean outlines, friendly characters and print-ready layouts that work on any home printer.

Questions

What age is the book for?

It is designed for children aged 3-6, with bold lines and large areas that are easy to color.

Is this a physical book?

No. It is a digital PDF that you download and print yourself. Nothing is shipped.

What paper size does it use?

US Letter (8.5 x 11 in). On A4 paper, choose "Fit to Page" in your print settings.

Can I print it more than once?

Yes. You may print it for your own children, family or classroom. Please do not resell, share or redistribute the file.
